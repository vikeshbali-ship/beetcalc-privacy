Privacy Policy

Beet Load Calculator · last updated 6 October 2026

The short version

Beet Load Calculator does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data. It has no internet access and requests no permissions.

What the app does

You type four numbers taken off a weighbridge: gross weight, tare weight, dirt tare and sugar percentage. The app works out the net weight, the dirt deducted, the clean beet weight and the sugar, and shows them on screen. The arithmetic happens entirely on your device.

Data we collect

None. Specifically:

  • No personal or identifying information is collected.
  • The numbers you type are held in memory while the app is open and are discarded when it closes. They are not written to storage.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or advertising libraries are included.
  • No account or sign-in is required or offered.

Permissions

The app declares no Android permissions at all. It cannot access the internet, your files, your location, your camera, your microphone or your contacts, because it has not asked for the ability to.

Data shared with third parties

None. There are no third-party services, software development kits or servers involved. Nothing leaves the device.

Children

The app is a calculator for people working with agricultural weighbridges. It collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app collects anything, this page will be updated before that version is published, and the date at the top will change.
